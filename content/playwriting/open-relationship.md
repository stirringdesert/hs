A documentary queer drama about finding oneself in a space where the boundaries of love are blurred and honesty is the hardest challenge. Through candid monologues and descriptions of everyday reality, the text explores the complexities of breaking away from conventional roles and monogamous expectations, turning intimate experiences into political and personal drama.

Staged reading, Reforum Space Budva, Montenegro (February 2025)

Staged reading, Munich (October 2025, directed by: Alexander Löwen, translation: David Drevs)
