What if we live in a multiverse, and around us countless sequential worlds are constantly being born, each containing a different version of us?

What if digital immortality becomes real, and we can upload our consciousness into a computer?

With each passing year, computers become more and more advanced, giving us the ability to experience what once seemed impossible — to feel like heroes of myths, conquerors of space, to embark on an incredible journey. But can the virtual world become a full replacement for the real one, and artificial intelligence — a new president?

Perfection for social media, a thrilling picture and VR instead of an ordinary world full of injustice, pain, fear, and the race for success. Who controls our lives? Have you ever thought about how digital technologies affect our consciousness?

Year after year, we create the illusion of a perfect life, but sooner or later we will have to accept not only life's imperfection, but also the inevitability of death.

Cast: Vladimir Kuznetsov, Niyolė Meilutė, Yulia Grishaeva, Alexander Khudyakov, Leon Slovitsky, Varya Svetlova, Alexander Lushin, Artur Fedynko, Vassa Bokova/Alexandra Zharova, Stanislav Belozerov, Yana Obroskova.
