At the heart of the production is the story of Charlie Gordon, a young intellectually disabled man who is offered an operation to increase his intelligence, which turns his entire life upside down. Reimagined and told by young directors, this story takes on a new and relevant resonance. The creators of the production speak about the search for meaning, about dreams and the collapse of illusions, about the clash between our perceptions and reality, and provoke the audience with many questions: What is normal? What can push us out of our comfort zone and stereotypes of perception? And do we even need it? Can one person change reality?
