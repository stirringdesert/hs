A production based on real events from the life of William Stanley Milligan — one of the most famous people in the history of psychiatry diagnosed with "multiple personality disorder." Billy Milligan was charged with committing a series of serious crimes, after which he was placed in a psychiatric hospital for medical examinations and to await his court verdict. Criminal or victim? Actor or madman? Can 24 personalities coexist within one person at the same time?
