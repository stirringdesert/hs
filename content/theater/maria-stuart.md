A tragicomedy based on documentary materials about the life of the last Catholic Queen of Scotland, Mary Stuart.

Against the backdrop of a religious schism among the people of Scotland, Mary Stuart becomes queen. She will face the trial of power and a confrontation with the English Queen Elizabeth, who ascended to the throne at the same time.

This production is not a historical reconstruction of past events, but rather a timeless story of inner struggle and the duality of human nature. The play "Mary Stuart" is not a legend covered in museum dust, but a story about us today, making our choices every day.
